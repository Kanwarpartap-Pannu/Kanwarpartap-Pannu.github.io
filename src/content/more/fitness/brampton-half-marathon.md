If you've watched *How I Met Your Mother*, you might remember an episode where Barney boasts about how anyone can run a marathon, no training needed, and so they hold him to it, and he actually finishes it.

In a similar spirit, my friend and I took on a slightly less daring challenge: to rawdog a half marathon. With only about two training runs before the marathon, we were going in completely untrained, but we pulled through and managed to finish. I came out with a 2:26 time. For my first half marathon, it was not too bad.

If you remember the episode, you might also remember that Barney completely loses function of his legs and has to call Ted to help him off the subway; he even gets his medal stolen. Well, we shared a very similar fate, being almost stuck to the bed the whole day afterwards.
