My first days learning to paint.

**Day 1:** I kept it simple with a page of fruit studies, a mango, an apple, a banana, and a slice of watermelon, to practise mixing colours, building up form, and placing cast shadows.

**Day 2:** I tried a wider mix of subjects on one page: a lemon with its leaf, a lion cub, and a jug sitting on red cloth.
